### Viral Content Analysis

**Platform Priority** (confirmed by research):
1. **TikTok** — #TravelTok has 200B+ views. 60% of Gen Z/millennials use TikTok as primary trip planning tool. Travel is one of TikTok's strongest categories.
2. **Instagram Reels** — 30.81% follower reach rate (highest of any IG format). Saves/DM shares are the #1 algorithm signal.
3. **Facebook** — low priority, link-click driver only.

**Video Length Sweet Spots:**
- TikTok: 21–34 seconds (short hooks), up to 60s for demos
- IG Reels: 15–30 seconds (26s = highest comment rate)
- The 70% completion rate threshold on TikTok means shorter = easier to promote

**The 5-Part Demo Structure (for Tripva screen recordings):**
1. **Hook** (0–3s) — destination hero shot + bold claim ("7 days in Bali, planned in 12 seconds")
2. **Problem** (3–7s) — "Stop opening 20 tabs" or pain point flash
3. **Demo** (7–20s) — screen record of tripva.app generating the plan, zoomed in, cut every 2-3s
4. **Proof** (20–25s) — show the finished itinerary dashboard, budget, photo spots
5. **CTA** (final 5s) — "Save this for your next trip" (drives saves) or "Link in bio to try free"

**Top Hook Templates for Tripva:**

| Hook Type | Template | Why It Works |
|-----------|----------|-------------|
| POV | "POV: you just planned 7 days in [destination] in 30 seconds" | Emotional placement, curiosity |
| Pain point | "Stop planning trips with 20 browser tabs" | Relatability, comment driver |
| Discovery | "This AI planned my entire [destination] trip — here's what it made" | Curiosity gap |
| Cost/budget | "I planned a $2,000 trip in 30 seconds — here's the full breakdown" | Save-bait |
| Bold claim | "[Destination] in 30 seconds. Not a summary. A full itinerary." | Specificity hooks attention |
| Urgency | "Watch this before you book your next trip" | FOMO/authority |

**Critical Production Rules:**
- **Raw > polished**: 31% higher engagement for authentic, unpolished content
- **Never show full UI**: Use extreme zoom on key moments (result appearing = "money shot", 150-200% zoom + half-second hold)
- **Cut all dead time**: No loading screens, no cursor wandering, jump from click to result
- **No logos/intros first 5 seconds**: Kills retention immediately
- **Native vertical (9:16)**: Never crop horizontal. No watermarks.
- **Platform-specific edits**: TikTok = snappy, slightly unpolished, higher energy. IG Reels = polished, save-worthy info.
